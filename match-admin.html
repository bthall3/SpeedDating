Compute & Send Matches

Step 1 loads the scanned scorecards into REDCap. Step 2 runs after all scorecards for an event are in. Both are safe to re-run.

1 · Import scanned scorecards

Choose the ratings CSV from the Speed Dating Scanner (eventNN_ratings.csv). Preview checks everything and writes nothing. Re-importing a corrected CSV updates cards in place, so there are no duplicates.

2 · Compute & send matches

Already-notified people are skipped, so run this only after step 1 is finished and checked. Tip: Download CSV Summary first to look at the matches without emailing anyone.
